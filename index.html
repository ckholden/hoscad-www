What HOSCAD does
📡

Live Unit Board

Every unit's status in real time — dispatched, en route, on scene, transporting, at hospital. Color-coded, sortable, searchable. Updates every 10 seconds.

🚑

Interfacility Dispatch

Full transport lifecycle from dispatch through handoff. Six milestone timestamps per call. Clinical type codes for CCT, IFT-ALS, IFT-BLS, discharge, dialysis, and more.

📱

Field MDT App

Crews log on from any phone or tablet. Status updates, destination selection, patient count, GPS tracking. Works offline. iOS and Android.

🏥

Diversion & Destinations

One-command diversion toggle per facility. Board and field app reflect diversion status instantly. Viewer mode for receiving facility staff.

🤝

Mutual Aid Coordination

Request, acknowledge, and release mutual aid from partner agencies directly from the board. Full audit trail. Automatic broadcast to dispatchers.

✈️

Air Resource Tracking

Live ADS-B telemetry for air medical helicopters and fixed-wing aircraft. Inbound alerts, altitude, speed, and heading on the board map.

🗺️

Live Map

Unit positions, incident scenes, hospital helipads, and aircraft on one map. Geocoding via OpenStreetMap. LOC command to set unit positions manually.

📊

Shift Reports & KPIs

Per-shift and per-unit call reports exportable to Excel. Response time analysis, unit utilization, call type distribution. No third-party BI tools needed.

🔒

Secure by Design

Role-based access for dispatchers, supervisors, managers, and field crews. SHA-256 session auth, brute-force protection, full audit trail on every state change.
